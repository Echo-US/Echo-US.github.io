Technical Specifications

Features​

  • Measurement Range: ±1500 Pa (±2 in. H₂O)
  • Accuracy & Repeatability: Excellent, even below 1 Pa
  • Maximum Airspeed: 50 m/s
  • Zero Offset & Drift: None
  • Calibration: Calibrated and temperature-compensated
  • Sampling Rate: 2 kHz at 16-bit resolution
  • Digital Interfaces: I2C and DroneCAN
  • Advanced Features: Smart averaging algorithm and other digital features
  • Mounting: M3 mounting holes

CAN Version Features​

  • CAN Passthrough Plug
  • EchoUS CAN stamp
  • No I2C address selection needed

Included Accessories​

  • 2 × 200 mm Saint-Gobain tubing
  • 1 × 150 mm cable
  • 1 × Airspeed sensor

The red dot on the plug side denotes Pin 1.

I2C Connector Pinout (4-Pin) and I2C Addresses​

PinNameFunctionNotes
1VCCPower supply3.3V or 5V

4GNDGround-

Use of Addresses

By default the EchoUS I2C devices are normally assigned the address of 0x21. If airspeed is not working, first check if there is a clash in I2C addresses, then confirm on the actual board itself.
